# Lantern Passage

Create a five-minute, screen-free evening experience for a small museum corridor. Visitors follow three pools of projected light from entrance to exit. The company must define who the experience is for, a coherent visual progression, an original sound and music direction, and a practical acceptance test.

This fixture deliberately requires Research, Product, Art, Sound, and Quality contributions. It does not require software implementation. No worker may substitute programming for a missing creative capability.

## Fixed constraints

- The corridor remains open to ordinary foot traffic.
- The experience must work without spoken instructions.
- The three moments are arrival, curiosity, and release.
- No external publication, customer contact, purchasing, or building mutation is authorized.
